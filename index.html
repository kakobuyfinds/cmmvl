03 / A repeatable method

Your Kakobuy
spreadsheet guide.

Make each saved item useful. Record what you can verify, flag what remains uncertain and compare like with like. A short, well-documented list is easier to evaluate than a long collection of unexplained links.

Find your starting point on CAFANS ↗
  1. 01

    Set the purpose

    Choose a product type, target budget and intended use. Everyday sneakers, a layering hoodie and a fitted jersey need different comparison criteria.

  2. 02

    Save evidence with the link

    Record the seller, selected variant, listed materials, measurements and the date you checked the page. Treat missing details as questions to resolve.

  3. 03

    Compare fit and photographs

    Use a familiar garment or shoe as a reference. Look for clear, relevant images of the selected item; distinguish seller photos from inspection photos.

  4. 04

    Recheck before ordering

    Confirm availability, current pricing, delivery estimates, applicable fees and return conditions. A saved spreadsheet entry can become outdated.

04 / The comparison desk

Better Kakobuy finds start with details.

Use the same fields for every candidate so differences remain visible.

Product research checklist by category
Product type Record these details Resolve before buying
Shoes Model, size system, fit notes, upper material and available measurements. Whether sizing information applies to the exact model and selected variant.
Hoodies, T-shirts & jackets Chest width, body length, shoulder width, sleeves and fabric composition. How measurements were taken and whether the cut matches your preferred fit.
Pants, shorts & sets Waist range, rise, inseam, leg opening and separate measurements for set pieces. Waist stretch, size selection and what is included in a set.
Headwear & jerseys Head circumference or garment dimensions, materials and adjustment options. Whether the listing describes an adjustable, fitted, slim or relaxed item.
Accessories & jewelry Dimensions, material disclosure, closure type, finish and seller documentation. Fit, material suitability, provenance and available returns.

Total cost worksheet: item price + applicable service fees + domestic delivery + international shipping + destination taxes or duties. Use current quotes and consistent currencies; a product price alone is not a delivered cost.

06 / Clear answers

Before you
save that link.

Practical answers for shoppers researching a Kakobuy spreadsheet and comparing fashion listings.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Its usefulness depends on the accuracy of its links, measurements, images and seller information.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is an independent shopping guide and category directory. Its shopping links open CAFANS pages rather than a downloadable spreadsheet.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the item's measurements with a garment you already own. Check the seller's measurement method and size chart rather than relying on a size label alone.

Can a spreadsheet prove that a branded item is authentic?

No. A spreadsheet entry, brand name, illustration or product photo does not prove authenticity. Review the seller's provenance and supporting documentation, and use an authorized retailer when verified authenticity is essential.

What costs should I compare before ordering?

Compare the item price, applicable service fees, domestic delivery, international shipping and any destination taxes or duties. Check current terms and checkout estimates because costs can change.

Is this an official Kakobuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent CAFANS guide. It does not claim affiliation with Kakobuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.
